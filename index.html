Smart ID

Completed

Group project · C++, Qt, OpenCV · Supervised by Er. Dinesh Gothe

A desktop app that generates Student, Business, and Library ID cards from one dynamic form. You can upload or capture a photo and signature, every field is validated, and cards are previewed and saved in per-person folders. It runs offline, so photos never leave your computer. Smart ID

Group project · C++, Qt, OpenCV · Khwopa College of Engineering

Smart ID is a desktop application that creates Student, Business, and Library ID cards from one dynamic form. It runs offline, validates every field, fits photos and signatures without distortion, and saves each card in its own folder. Built by Alwina Shakya, Muskan Sahani, Anjan Garauja, Dibesh Nagarkoti, and Alvin Karmacharya, and presented at the project defense on 10 August 2026.
